Top Places in Hunza

Discover the beautiful places of Hunza Valley.

Attabad Lake

Attabad Lake is a beautiful lake with blue water and mountains all around it. Visitors can enjoy boating and take amazing pictures here. The most beautiful thing is its bright blue water.

Altit Fort

Altit Fort is a historical place in Hunza. It is famous for its old buildings and amazing views of the valley. It is a great place to learn about the history of Hunza.

Some helpful information before visiting Hunza.

What is the best time to visit Hunza?

April to October is a popular time to visit Hunza. The weather is usually pleasant, and visitors can enjoy the mountains and beautiful scenery.
